What you can make

How everything works →
→

Autopilot

One line in. Gobo writes the shots, each is keyframed and animated, the cuts are checked, the film is cut.

 →

Steal the look

Drop any frame. Rust reads its tone, colour, grain and letterbox, names the stock and grade, and hands you the .cube.

 →

Draw the move

Scribble on the frame: a loop orbits, a spiral dives into the eyes, a fast flick whips. The action writes itself.

 →

Grade like film

Portra, Tungsten 500T, bleach bypass: live on your clip with halation and moving grain, every frame in Rust.

 →

Direct a shot

Camera, lens, framing, light, film, look and move become the prompt a DP would write.

 →

Shootout

One shot on up to four models at once, side by side, to see which one gets it.

 →

Board

Brief in, shot list out: keyframe every shot, animate them, and cut the spot. The script supervisor flags every jump.

 →

Footage

Bring in a clip you shot, then restyle it, swap something in it, or make it longer.

 →

Library

Everything you've made, across every reel, searchable and ready to download.
